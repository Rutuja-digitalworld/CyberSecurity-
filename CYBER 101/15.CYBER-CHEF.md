
# HANDS ON PRACTICE:
## Q1. What is the hidden email address?

### Steps:

1. Open **CyberChef**.
2. Load the downloaded **Extractor `.txt` file** into the **Input** section.
3. On the left side, search for:
   **Extract email addresses**
4. Click/drag **Extract email addresses** into the **Recipe** section.
5. Look at the **Output** section.
6. Copy the email address shown there.
7. Submit that email as your answer.

**Answer:** `hidden@hotmail.com`

## Q2. What is the hidden IP address that ends in `.232`?

### Steps:

1. Remove/disable **Extract email addresses** from the Recipe.
2. In the left **Operations** search box, type:
   **Extract IP addresses**
3. Add **Extract IP addresses** to the Recipe.
4. Look at the **Output** section.
5. You may see more than one IP address.
6. Find the IP address that ends with **`.232`**.
7. Submit that IP address.

**Answer:** `10.10.2.10`

# Q4. What is the binary value of decimal number `78`?

This one does **not** require the downloaded file.

### Steps in CyberChef:

1. Remove the previous operation from the Recipe.
2. Search for **From Decimal**.
3. Add **From Decimal** to the Recipe.
4. In the **Input** box, type:

```text
78
```

5. Check the **Output**.

### Answer:

```text
01001110
```

# Q5. What is the URL encoded value of `https://tryhackme.com/r/careers`?

### Steps:

1. Remove the previous operation from the Recipe.
2. Search for:
   **URL Encode**
3. Add **URL Encode** to the Recipe.
4. In the **Input** box, paste:

```text
https://tryhackme.com/r/careers
```

5. Check the **Output** section.
6. Copy the encoded value.
7. Submit it.

### Answer:

```text
https%3A%2F%2Ftryhackme.com%2Fr%2Fcareers
```
<img width="1600" height="531" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-09-19 at 21 01 05" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/57c2516f-591b-4b21-aca5-0d91ac3844d1" />

The task specifically explains that `:` becomes `%3A` and `/` becomes `%2F` during URL encoding.
