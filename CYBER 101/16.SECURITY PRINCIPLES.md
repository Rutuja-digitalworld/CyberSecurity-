# 🔐 Security Principles 

# TASK 1: CIA Security Triad

The **CIA Triad** is the foundation of information security. It consists of:

<img width="778" height="695" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f14f9b13-77de-4bff-a7e4-6f894b18069c" />


| Principle              | Meaning                                                                              | Example                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🔒 **Confidentiality** | Only authorized people can access information.                                       | Credit card details should only be accessible to the payment processor.              |
| 🛡️ **Integrity**      | Data should not be modified without authorization, and changes should be detectable. | An online order's shipping address should not be changed by an attacker.             |
| ⚡ **Availability**     | Systems and data should be available when required.                                  | An online shopping website should be accessible when customers want to place orders. |

----------------------
### 🔒 Confidentiality

* Prevents **unauthorized access or disclosure** of information.
* Protects sensitive information such as:

  * Passwords
  * Credit card details
  * Medical records
  * Personal information

**Example:** A healthcare provider must protect a patient's medical records from unauthorized people.

----------------------

### 🛡️ Integrity

* Ensures that data remains **accurate and unchanged** unless an authorized modification is made.
* Unauthorized modification should also be **detectable**.

**Example:** If an attacker changes the shipping address of an online order, the package may be delivered to the wrong person.

-------------------------------

### ⚡ Availability

* Ensures that systems, services, and information are **accessible when needed**.

**Example:** If an online shopping website is down, customers cannot browse products or place orders.

> **Note:** All three CIA principles do not always have equal importance.
> For example, a university announcement may not need confidentiality, but its **integrity is very important**.

---

## 2. CIA in Different Systems

### 🛒 Online Shopping

**Confidentiality:**
Customer payment and personal information should not be disclosed to unauthorized parties.

**Integrity:**
Order details such as quantity, price, and shipping address should not be modified illegally.

**Availability:**
The website/app should remain available so customers can browse and place orders.

### 🏥 Healthcare System

**Confidentiality:**
Patient medical records must be protected from unauthorized disclosure.

**Integrity:**
Incorrectly modified medical records can result in incorrect treatment.

**Availability:**
Doctors need access to patient records when treating patients.

---

# 3. Beyond CIA

Security also includes **Authenticity** and **Nonrepudiation**.

### ✅ Authenticity

**Authenticity** ensures that the data, file, message, or request is actually from the **claimed source**.

**Example:**
A company receiving an order for **1000 cars** needs to verify that the order was genuinely placed by the claimed customer.

--------------------

### ✍️ Nonrepudiation

**Nonrepudiation** ensures that the original sender **cannot later deny** having created or sent the data.

**Example:**
If a customer places an order for 1000 cars, the company should have evidence proving that the customer actually placed the order.

### 🔗 Difference

| Concept            | Main Question                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Authenticity**   | "Is this really from the claimed source?"  |
| **Nonrepudiation** | "Can the source deny sending/creating it?" |

---

## 4. Parkerian Hexad

The **Parkerian Hexad**, proposed by **Donn Parker in 1998**, extends security into **six elements**.

### 🔷 Six Elements

1. **Availability**
2. **Utility**
3. **Integrity**
4. **Authenticity**
5. **Confidentiality**
6. **Possession**

### 📌 Utility

**Utility** means that information must be in a **useful and usable form**.

**Example:**
A laptop contains encrypted data, but the owner has lost the decryption key.

* Data is still present ✅
* Laptop is available ✅
* But the information cannot be used ❌

Therefore, the information has **no utility** to the user.

### 📦 Possession

**Possession** means protecting information from **unauthorized taking, copying, or control**.

**Example 1:**
An attacker steals a backup drive containing important data.

**Example 2:**
Ransomware encrypts an organization's files and prevents the organization from controlling/accessing them.

In both cases, the organization can lose **possession/control of its information**.

## TRY HACK ME VIEW SITE FLAQ QUESTIONS 

**Q1.** You went to cash out a cheque, and the bank teller made you wait for five minutes as they confirmed the signature of the cheque's issuer. Which security function is the bank teller checking?
**Answer:** **Authenticity**

**Q2.** At a police checkpoint, the police officer suspected that the vehicle registration papers were fake. Which security function does the officer think is lacking?
**Answer:** **Authenticity**

**Q3.** As the troops got deployed, the leader stressed that they should not communicate their location to anyone while the mission was ongoing. Which security function did the leader want to have?
**Answer:** **Confidentiality**

**Q4.** One hotel is stressing that the Internet over its WiFi network must be accessible 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Which security pillar is the hotel requiring?
**Answer:** **Availability**

**Q5.** Two companies are negotiating a certain agreement; however, they want to keep the details of the agreement secret. Which security pillar are they emphasizing?
**Answer:** **Confidentiality**

# TASK 2:🔐 DAD Triad 
<img width="718" height="636" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87eb9f82-c291-450b-ad53-5358ed4b625d" />

The **DAD Triad** represents attacks against the three principles of the **CIA Triad**.

| CIA Principle       | Opposite Attack          | Meaning                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| **Confidentiality** | **Disclosure**           | Unauthorized access or exposure of data |
| **Integrity**       | **Alteration**           | Unauthorized modification of data       |
| **Availability**    | **Destruction / Denial** | Making data or systems unavailable      |

### 🔓 Disclosure

**Disclosure** is an attack against **Confidentiality**.

* Occurs when confidential or private information is accessed or exposed by an unauthorized person.
* The attacker may steal data and make it publicly available.

**Example:**
An attacker gains access to customer records and **dumps them online**.

➡️ **Attack: Disclosure**

---

### ✏️ Alteration

**Alteration** is an attack against **Integrity**.

* Occurs when data is **modified without authorization**.
* The altered information may cause incorrect decisions or actions.

**Example:**
An attacker modifies a patient's medical record, causing a doctor to provide the wrong treatment.

➡️ **Attack: Alteration**

---

### 💥 Destruction / Denial

**Destruction/Denial** is an attack against **Availability**.

* Occurs when data, systems, or services are **destroyed or made unavailable**.
* It prevents legitimate users from accessing the required resources.

**Example:**
Attackers switch off both the main and backup power supplies, causing the entire network to shut down.

➡️ **Attack: Denial**

---

## 2. DAD Triad and Patient Records

### Disclosure

If an attacker steals patient records and publishes them online, the healthcare provider suffers a **confidentiality breach**.

### Alteration

If an attacker modifies patient records, doctors may provide incorrect treatment. This attacks **integrity** and can potentially be life-threatening.

### Destruction / Denial

If attackers make the medical database unavailable, doctors cannot access patient records and the facility may be unable to operate properly.

---

## 3. Balance Between CIA Principles

Security requires a **balance between Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability**.

* Excessive **Confidentiality and Integrity** controls can make information difficult to access, reducing **Availability**.
* Increasing **Availability** too much can sometimes weaken **Confidentiality and Integrity**.
* Therefore, security controls should be designed to maintain an appropriate balance between all three principles.

--------------------

### Answers

**Q1.** The attacker managed to gain access to customer records and dumped them online. What is this attack?
**Answer:** **Disclosure**

**Q2.** A group of attackers were able to locate both the main and the backup power supply systems and switch them off. As a result, the whole network was shut down. What is this attack?
**Answer:** **Denial**
