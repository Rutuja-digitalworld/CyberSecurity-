## **📌 TASK 1 — What is Security Awareness?**

**Security Awareness:** Knowledge of cyber threats, risks and safe practices to protect individuals and organizations from cyberattacks.

**Why is it important?**

- Helps identify common cyberattacks.
- Teaches how to mitigate potential threats.
- Improves cyber hygiene.
- Protects organizational data and systems.

# **95%**

**Human Errors — IBM Report**

Human errors were the main reason for 95% of successful cyberattacks.

Key point: Humans can become the weakest link in organizational security.

### **👥 Who is Security Awareness For?**

**EVERYONE!** It is everyone's responsibility, regardless of their job role, because anyone with organizational access or knowledge can become a target for cybercriminals.

## **📌 TASK 2 — Why Security Awareness is Essential?**

### **1. Growing Cybersecurity Risks**

- Remote working is common in organizations.
- Employees often use personal computers and home networks.
- This increases exposure to cyberattacks.
- Hackers use various tools and techniques to access employee computers and corporate networks.
